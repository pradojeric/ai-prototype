# Strings v2.0 - Game Design Updates

## Core Concept Shifts

The new iteration moves away from static, menu-based riddle encounters and integrates Filipino cultural trivia directly into active 3D combat. To preserve the atmospheric pacing, Combat Encounters are now instanced in separate spaces (**Memory Arenas**). Upon victory, the player returns to the main zone to find the artifacts scattered in the real world. It also introduces "**Guardian Souls**" as a definitive endgame goal.

## The New Game Loop

1. **Descend & Explore (Main Zone):** Enter the submerged zone. The player wades through the atmospheric environment. Artifacts do not exist here yet. The goal is to locate a glowing "Memory Rift" or gateway.
2. **Transition to the Memory Arena:** Interacting with the Rift teleports the player to a separate, isolated 3D space designed specifically for the combat encounter.
3. **The Zone Challenge (Arena):** Player engages in a zone-specific action sequence (Wave Defense, Rail Shooter, or Tower Ascension). The Guardian is an active participant/threat in this realm, supported by zone-specific lesser enemies.
4. **Active Riddle Combat (Arena):** During the challenge, the Guardian attacks using riddles (*bugtong*). The player must solve the riddle within the 3D environment using their aim or movement to deal damage and survive.
5. **Victory & Return:** Defeating the Guardian collapses the arena. The player is teleported back to the Main Zone.
6. **Soul & Artifact Collection (Main Zone):** Back in the original zone, the "Guardian Soul" and the zone's cultural artifacts are now scattered across the map. The player gathers them peacefully using the String mechanic.
7. **The Museum Hub:** Player returns to the Digital Museum to display artifacts.
8. **The Final Memory:** After clearing all three zones, the player places the three collected Guardian Souls into a central pedestal to trigger a definitive, scripted ending sequence.

## Combat Drops (Memory Lumina)

Defeating lesser enemies within the Memory Arenas has a chance to drop temporary glowing orbs called Lumina. Players can shoot these orbs or run over them to gain immediate buffs necessary for surviving the escalating difficulty:

- **Green Lumina (Vitality):** Restores a portion of the player's (or boat's) HP.
- **Blue Lumina (Zephyr):** Grants Infinite Sprint and increased movement speed for 8 seconds. Crucial for outrunning the rising water in Zone 3 or dodging Zone 1 swarms.
- **Gold Lumina (Overcharge):** Upgrades the player's light-bolt into a rapid-fire machine gun with no cooldown for 5 seconds. Excellent for clearing heavy enemy waves.

## Zone-Specific Mechanics (Memory Arenas)

### Zone 1: PONSIA (Food Zone) - "Riddle Breakers"

- **The Arena:** An enclosed, circular spectral kitchen/marketplace surrounded by a void.
- **Challenge:** Defend the center against waves of enemies by casting light-bolts.
- **Zone Enemies:**
  - **Starved Fishers (Melee):** Fast-moving, skeletal echoes that swarm the player in large numbers.
  - **Brine Spitters (Ranged):** Stationary enemies that lob slow-moving, corrosive salt projectiles from the edges of the arena.
- **Guardian Threat:** The Feastkeeper appears with invincible armor.
- **The Riddle:** The Guardian speaks a riddle. Three glowing, breakable coral nodes spawn, each displaying an answer.
- **Action:** The player must shoot the correct node while dodging enemies. A correct hit breaks the Guardian's armor; a wrong hit spawns an immediate wave of Starved Fishers.

### Zone 2: LIKET (Festival Zone) - "Riddle Volley"

- **The Arena:** A fast-moving, spectral river surrounded by floating festival lights and darkness.
- **Challenge:** A pure rail shooter sequence. The player is locked in place on a moving traditional fishing boat driving rapidly down the river. The player cannot move their character; they must survive entirely by aiming and shooting to defend the boat.
- **Zone Enemies:**
  - **River Snipers (Ranged):** Echoes perched on floating debris and riverbanks. They fire fast, standard projectiles that the player must continuously shoot down to preserve the boat's HP.
  - **Frenzied Boarders (Melee):** Leaping echoes that land directly on the boat. Since the player cannot move away, these must be blasted off immediately before they deal heavy damage to the vessel.
- **Guardian Threat:** The Reveler floats ahead of the boat and periodically attacks with a riddle barrage.
- **The Riddle:** The Guardian speaks a riddle and throws three distinct projectiles (e.g., festival lanterns/masks) at the boat simultaneously. Each projectile displays a different answer via a 3D sprite.
- **Action:** In the locked first-person view, the player must quickly aim and shoot the correct incoming lantern with a light-bolt before it hits the boat. A correct shot deflects the energy back to damage the Guardian. Wrong or missed shots severely damage the boat.

### Zone 3: PANANISIA (Landmark Zone) - "Tower Ascension"

- **The Arena:** The hollowed, towering interior of a massive ruin (inspired by the Cape Bolinao Lighthouse or Provincial Capitol).
- **Challenge:** A vertical climbing sequence. A lethal tide of dark, corrupted water steadily rises from the bottom of the tower. The player must continuously climb ramps and spiral stairs to avoid drowning.
- **Zone Enemies:**
  - **Gargoyle Sentinels (Heavy Melee):** Stone enemies that spawn on narrow staircases. They physically block the path and take multiple light-bolts to shatter, forcing the player to waste precious time while the water rises.
  - **Gale Whispers (Ranged Evasive):** Flying enemies that circle the central shaft of the tower, shooting projectiles to knock the player off balance.
- **Guardian Threat:** The Archivist manifests as a giant entity hovering in the center of the tower, controlling the water level.
- **The Riddle:** Periodically, the stairs are completely blocked by a massive, locked gate with three ancient mechanisms (the answers).
- **Action:** Under the extreme pressure of the rising water, the player must shoot the correct mechanism to unlock the gate and continue climbing. A wrong choice spawns Gargoyle Sentinels and temporarily slows the player's movement speed, heavily risking them being swallowed by the rising tide.

## Endgame: The Final Memory

- **The Goal:** The Museum hub receives a central altar/pedestal.
- **The Resolution:** Placing the three Guardian Souls into this pedestal triggers the final cutscene. This sequence pieces together Hil's lost memories and the truth of the sunken province.
